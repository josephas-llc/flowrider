## TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

### Video
- **Resolution:** 1920x1080 (16:9) or 1080x1080 (1:1 for social)
- **Frame Rate:** 60fps for smooth icosahedron rotation
- **Codec:** H.264 or H.265

### Audio
- **Voiceover:** Clear, confident, slightly fast-paced
- **Music:** Subtle lo-fi synthwave, under VO
- **Levels:** VO at -6dB, music at -18dB

### Recording Setup
- Use macOS screen recording (QuickTime or OBS)
- Hide personal bookmarks and notifications
- Use Demo Mode in app (pre-populated data)
- Have 6-8 sessions pre-configured with realistic names
