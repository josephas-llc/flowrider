### Cursor Timeline (for reference)

| Date | Milestone | Valuation |
|------|-----------|-----------|
| 2022 | Founded by MIT/OpenAI engineers | - |
| Mar 2023 | YC W23 batch | ~$10M |
| Aug 2024 | Series A | $400M |
| Dec 2024 | Series B | $2.5B |
| May 2025 | Series C | $9B |
| Nov 2025 | Series D | $29.3B |
| Jun 2026 | SpaceX acquisition | **$60B** |

### What We Learn from Cursor

1. **Speed matters** - They moved from $0 to $4B ARR in 3 years
2. **AI premium is real** - 50x revenue multiples possible
3. **Talent density** - Small team (100 people at $4B ARR)
4. **Product-led growth** - Zero marketing spend to $100M ARR
5. **Enterprise conversion** - Self-serve to enterprise works

### Our Differentiated Path

| Factor | Cursor | Flowrider |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| Focus | Single AI agent | Multi-agent orchestration |
| Sessions | 1 | 20-400 |
| Providers | OpenAI-centric | Model-agnostic |
