Frequently Asked Questions

How does the license key work?

After purchase, you'll receive a license key via email. Enter it in the Flowrider app (Settings → License) to unlock your tier. You can activate on up to 3 devices (10 for Team).

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes! Cancel your subscription anytime from your Stripe billing portal. You'll keep access until the end of your billing period.

What AI providers are supported?

Flowrider works with Claude, GPT-4, Gemini, Grok, Ollama, and any OpenAI-compatible API. Bring your own API keys.

Is my data stored locally?

Yes! All your code, sessions, and project data stay on your machine. We only validate your license key with our server.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes, we offer a 14-day money-back guarantee. If Flowrider isn't right for you, email hello@josephas.com for a full refund.
